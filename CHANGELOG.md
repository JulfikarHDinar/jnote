# Changelog

All notable changes to jNote are listed here. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-20

### Added
- Automatic backup, off until you turn it on in Settings > Backup. Pick Google Drive or a folder on the phone. When your notes change, leaving the app saves them all to that one file. Restore it with Import notes. jNote still has no internet permission: the Drive app does the upload.
- Settings > Check for updates opens the latest release on GitHub.

### Fixed
- A ringing reminder no longer goes quiet when you pull down the notifications. It rings until you tap Snooze, Dismiss or the reminder, or swipe it away. After 10 minutes it stops and stays as a quiet notification.
- In the calendar's export selection, This week and This month now select exactly that week or month instead of adding to what was already selected. Tapping one again clears it.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-14

First public release.

### Added
- Home-screen note bar in two sizes (3×1 and 2×1). It opens a small card: type, tap ✓, done.
- Today's timeline with times and bullets. Tap a note to edit it; swipe it away to delete, with Undo.
- Calendar with dots on days that have notes. Open any day to read or add notes.
- Reminders that ring like an alarm, with Snooze 10 min and Dismiss. They survive restarts and app updates.
- Plain-text export of any days: copy, share or save as a file.
- Import of saved jNote files, so notes can move to another phone.
- Six themes: three dark and three light.
- Unsent text in the typing bar and the widget's card is kept as a draft.
- Works fully offline: no account, no ads, no tracking and no internet permission.
