# Changelog

All notable changes to jNote are listed here. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-14

First public release.

### Added
- Home-screen note bar in two sizes (3×1 and 2×1). It opens a small card: type, tap ✓, done.
- Today's timeline with times and bullets. Tap a note to edit it; swipe it away to delete, with Undo.
- Calendar with dots on days that have notes. Open any day to read or add notes.
- Reminders that ring like an alarm, with Snooze 10 min and Dismiss. They survive restarts and app updates.
- Plain-text export of any days: copy, share or save as a file.
- Import of saved jNote files, so notes can move to another phone.
- Six themes: three dark and three light.
- Unsent text in the typing bar and the widget's card is kept as a draft.
- Works fully offline: no account, no ads, no tracking and no internet permission.
