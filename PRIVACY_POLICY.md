# jNote privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

jNote is a note-taking app that works fully offline.

**What jNote stores.** The notes you write, the time you wrote them, any reminder times you set, and two app settings (theme and first day of the week). All of it is stored only on your phone, in jNote's private storage.

**What jNote sends.** Nothing. jNote has no internet access, no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your notes are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

**Backups and new phones.** jNote's data is excluded from Android cloud backup. When you set up a new phone, Android can copy your notes across in a direct phone-to-phone transfer, which you control.

**Sharing, copying and files.** Notes leave the app only when you choose to: when you tap Share and pick another app, tap Copy, or save them as a text file where you choose. Import reads only the one file you pick in Android's file picker. jNote writes to the clipboard only when you tap Copy, and never reads the clipboard.

**Permissions.** Notifications and alarms are used only to ring the reminders you set. Restart access is used only to restore those reminders after your phone restarts.

**Deleting your data.** Delete a note by swiping it away or by using Delete in the editor, or delete every note in a date range from Settings > Delete notes. Uninstalling jNote, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of your notes.

**Children.** jNote is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
